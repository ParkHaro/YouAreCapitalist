## 1. Loop Architecture Overview

### 1.1 Three-Tier Loop System

```
📊 META-LOOP (Long-term Strategy)
├── 🔄 CORE-LOOP (Operational Decisions)  
│   ├── ⚡ MICRO-LOOP (Real-time Reactions)
│   ├── ⚡ MICRO-LOOP (Real-time Reactions)
│   └── ⚡ MICRO-LOOP (Real-time Reactions)
├── 🔄 CORE-LOOP (Operational Decisions)
└── 🔄 CORE-LOOP (Operational Decisions)
```

#### Micro-Loop (Seconds to Minutes)
- **Market observation** → **Quick decisions** → **Immediate results** → **Market response**
- Real-time stock trading, news reactions, emergency interventions
- High-frequency but low-impact decisions

#### Core-Loop (Minutes to Hours) 
- **Analysis** → **Strategy formulation** → **Implementation** → **Performance evaluation**
- Portfolio rebalancing, company management, investment decisions
- Medium-frequency, high-impact decisions

#### Meta-Loop (Days to Weeks)
- **Trend analysis** → **Strategic planning** → **System building** → **Empire expansion**
- Market positioning, automation setup, long-term investments
- Low-frequency, transformational decisions

### 1.2 Time System Integration

#### Real-Time Layer
- Market price fluctuations (every few seconds)
- Breaking news events (random intervals)
- Pop behavior changes (continuous)

#### Accelerated Time Layer
- Daily market close/open cycles
- Quarterly financial reports
- Annual economic trends

#### Strategic Time Layer
- Multi-year investment horizons
- Economic cycle phases
- Generational wealth transfer

## 2. Core Gameplay Loops

### 2.1 Investment Loop (Primary Economic Engine)

```
💰 CAPITAL ALLOCATION
↓
📊 MARKET ANALYSIS
├── Technical indicators
├── Fundamental analysis
├── Sentiment analysis
└── AI advisor recommendations
↓
🎯 INVESTMENT DECISION
├── Asset selection (stocks, bonds, derivatives)
├── Position sizing
├── Risk management
└── Timeline setting
↓
📈 POSITION MANAGEMENT
├── Performance monitoring
├── Rebalancing triggers
├── Exit strategy execution
└── Profit/loss realization
↓
💵 CAPITAL GROWTH/LOSS
├── Reinvestment decisions
├── Leverage adjustment
├── Diversification needs
└── Risk capacity changes
↓
(Loop back to CAPITAL ALLOCATION)
```

#### Key Decision Points:
- **Buy/Sell/Hold**: Based on market analysis and risk tolerance
- **Leverage Usage**: Risk vs. reward calculations
- **Portfolio Balance**: Diversification vs. concentration
- **Automation Level**: Manual control vs. algorithmic trading

## 3. Loop Evolution by Game Phase

### 3.1 Early Phase: Manual Operation (Assets < 1B)

**Primary Loops**: Investment Loop (80%) + Corporate Control (20%)

**Player Experience**:
- Direct stock trading and analysis
- Individual company research
- Manual portfolio management
- Learning fundamental concepts

**Decision Frequency**: High (every few minutes)
**Automation Level**: 0-20%

### 3.2 Mid Phase: Semi-Automation (Assets 1-10B)

**Primary Loops**: Corporate Control (50%) + Investment Loop (30%) + Capital Growth (20%)

**Player Experience**:
- CEO appointment and oversight
- Portfolio automation tools
- Multi-company management
- Leverage utilization

**Decision Frequency**: Medium (every hour)
**Automation Level**: 30-60%

### 3.3 Late Phase: Strategic Oversight (Assets 10B-1T)

**Primary Loops**: Capital Growth (40%) + Market Influence (30%) + Corporate Control (30%)

**Player Experience**:
- System-level strategy setting
- Political influence campaigns
- M&A orchestration
- Economic trend riding

**Decision Frequency**: Low (daily/weekly)
**Automation Level**: 60-90%

### 3.4 End Phase: Systemic Control (Assets > 1T)

**Primary Loops**: Market Influence (60%) + Capital Growth (40%)

**Player Experience**:
- Economic system manipulation
- Political outcome control
- Crisis management
- Legacy planning

**Decision Frequency**: Very Low (weekly/monthly)
**Automation Level**: 90-100%

## 4. Player Decision Framework

### 4.1 Decision Types by Impact and Frequency

| Decision Type | Frequency | Impact | Automation | Examples |
|---------------|-----------|---------|------------|----------|
| **Tactical** | High | Low | Full | Daily trades, news reactions |
| **Operational** | Medium | Medium | Partial | Portfolio rebalancing, CEO changes |
| **Strategic** | Low | High | Minimal | Market entry, major acquisitions |
| **Systemic** | Very Low | Very High | None | Crisis responses, legacy decisions |

### 4.2 Automation Progression

#### Level 0: Manual Control
- All decisions require player input
- Real-time market monitoring
- High engagement, steep learning curve

#### Level 1: Tactical Automation
- Automated day trading
- Stop-loss and limit orders
- Basic portfolio maintenance

#### Level 2: Operational Automation
- CEO performance management
- Systematic rebalancing
- Risk management protocols

#### Level 3: Strategic Automation
- Market timing algorithms
- M&A opportunity scanning
- Political influence campaigns

#### Level 4: Full Automation
- Self-optimizing capital allocation
- Autonomous growth strategies
- System-wide decision making

## 10. Technical Implementation Notes

### 10.1 Loop Processing Architecture
- **Micro-loops**: High-frequency, lightweight processing
- **Core-loops**: Balanced computation and storage
- **Meta-loops**: Complex calculations, persistent state

### 10.2 State Management
- **Real-time State**: Market prices, news events
- **Persistent State**: Portfolio holdings, relationships
- **Historical State**: Performance records, decision logs

### 10.3 Automation Framework
- **Rule-based Systems**: Simple condition-action pairs
- **AI Decision Making**: Machine learning for complex choices
- **Hybrid Approach**: Human oversight with AI assistance
